Honesty footnotes: our real Toy Computer spells CHECK as two instructions (a test and a jump), and MOVE by the program editing its own reading order — that story is rungs 5–6 of the Toy Computer. C also often marks a text's end with a 0 instead of counting (the null terminator — Week 10). And in a real machine there are more storeys between printf and the pixels (OS, drivers, fonts) — same idea, more floors. Below this floor, every WRITE is a bell-ring story of flip-flops and gates: see Under the Hood.
