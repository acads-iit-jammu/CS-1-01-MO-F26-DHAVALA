Why does 72 mean H? Because we all agreed it does — in 1963. A code is a convention. (Tomorrow's question, and Week 2's topic: how numbers themselves are represented.)

The machine's diary

The deepest line on this page: the value 72 is a quantity to the adder, the letter H to the display, and half an instruction to the decoder. Same bits — three meanings. Meaning lives in the reader, not in the bits.
